ESC — BACK TO THE ROOM

A Walkable Project Gallery

The White Room

Nine works hang in a single white cube. Walk up to a piece and click — the camera steps back and draws up alongside it, the work takes the left of the frame, its page slides in on the right, and closing walks you back to where you stood.

WASD move Move mouse — turn QE turn Scroll lean in Click view — stand close

Nothing automatic — the view turns only while the mouse is moving (Q / E also turn). The centre reticle is your aim: walk a work under it, click to view, Esc reverses the move.
